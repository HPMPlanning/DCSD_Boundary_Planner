    Welcome to the Community Boundary Builder

    This planner uses the Option A elementary school boundaries from Engagement Session 3. Each shaded area on the map is one Option A elementary zone. The elementary boundaries stay as they are. You decide which middle school and high school each zone attends.

    How to build a plan

    1. Pick the Middle school or High school tab.
    2. Choose a school in the side panel, then click zones on the map to assign them to it. Double-click a zone to unassign it.
    3. Watch each school's bar. The black mark is full capacity, and the percent turns red when a school is over.
    4. Use the High Achievers Program dropdown to pick which school hosts it. Its students count toward that school.
    5. When every zone has a middle and high school, select Review and submit plan.

    About "nearest school"

    The button that fills zones with the nearest school measures a straight line from the center point of each elementary zone to each school. It doesn't use driving distance or where students live within the zone, so treat it as a starting point and adjust from there.

    About the numbers

    On the map, each zone shows its Middle Students (grades 6 to 8) on the Middle school tab and its High Students (grades 9 to 12) on the High school tab: the students who live in that elementary zone.
